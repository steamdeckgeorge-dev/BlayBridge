BlayBridge Tools — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy applies to the BlayBridge Tools Android application (package name com.blaybridge.tools), published by BlayBridge. It is separate from the privacy policy for the BlayBridge website.

What the app does

BlayBridge Tools provides utilities for documents, PDF files, images, scanning, QR codes and related tasks. Some tools request access to the camera or to files you select so they can carry out your chosen task.

Camera, documents and files

Camera access is used for scanning when you choose to use camera-based features. Files and images you select may be read and processed to provide the requested functionality. The app uses Android file-sharing mechanisms to let you export or share results with apps you choose. Do not share sensitive documents with recipients you do not trust. App backup is disabled in the Android application configuration. We do not claim that all files are permanently stored or deleted on a fixed schedule; handling may vary by tool and Android's storage behaviour.

Advertising and third-party services

The app includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage applicable consent choices. These services may automatically process information such as advertising identifiers (where available), device and app information, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and compliance. Google may process this information under its own terms and privacy policy. For details see Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure guidance.

Other on-device processing

The Android project includes libraries for QR-code scanning, text recognition and PDF handling. The presence of these libraries does not by itself mean that your documents are sent to an external server. Processing and any network transfers depend on the feature and app version. We will update this policy if additional cloud processing, accounts or integrations are introduced.

Permissions

The app requests camera permission for camera-based tools and internet/network-state permissions for connected services including advertising. Access to individual documents may also be granted through Android's file selection interfaces. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

Children

BlayBridge Tools is a general-purpose utility app, not specifically designed for children. Any applicable age-related advertising and privacy controls must be applied in the app and Play Console configuration.

Your choices

You can decline optional permissions, adjust device advertising settings and use available advertising consent controls where presented. You can remove the app from your device. To ask about data processing or privacy rights, contact us using the address below.

Security and changes

We aim to limit access to the information needed to provide app features. No method of electronic processing or transmission is completely secure. This policy may be updated as app features or third-party services change; updates will be posted on this page.
