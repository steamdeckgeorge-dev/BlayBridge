Privacy and compatibility

AI generation happens locally on your device, rather than sending your text to a paid AI API. The browser downloads model files from a public model host and loads the AI library from a CDN. We do not save your prompts on BlayBridge's server.

Some older phones and browsers may be unable to run the model because of memory or device limitations. No AI service can guarantee perfectly accurate answers.
