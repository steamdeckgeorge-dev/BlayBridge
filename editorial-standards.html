BlayBridge Editorial Standards

BlayBridge is an independent publication edited by George Blay Buaben. This page explains how we handle sourcing, opinion, updates, corrections and AI assistance.

Research and sources

For factual articles, we link to the material used to support important claims. We prefer primary sources such as official records, research papers, company statements and data, and use established reporting for context. A link is provided so readers can inspect the source directly. A link does not mean the source endorses BlayBridge.

When a story relies on a report or public statement that has not been independently confirmed, we say who made the claim and what remains unconfirmed. We aim to correct broken or outdated links and revisit claims when material new information becomes available.

Reporting and opinion

BlayBridge publishes explainers, practical guides, reporting and commentary. Opinions represent the author’s analysis and are not presented as established fact. Readers should be able to tell when an article is discussing a point of view rather than reporting a confirmed event.

Authorship and AI assistance

Articles identify their author on the page. AI tools may assist with brainstorming, research organization or editing. They are not treated as sources or as a substitute for checking factual claims. The editor is responsible for the final text, its sources and any corrections.

Updates and corrections

When we make a meaningful factual update, we update the article’s revision information where available. If you find an error, email blaybridge@gmail.com with the article link and the correction you believe is needed. We review correction requests and amend the page when the evidence supports a change. Substantive corrections should be made visible to readers.

Contact

Questions about our editorial work can be sent to blaybridge@gmail.com. You can also read About BlayBridge, our Privacy Policy and our Terms.
