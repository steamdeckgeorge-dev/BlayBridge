Privacy Policy

BlayBridge Tools Android app · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how BlayBridge handles information in the BlayBridge Tools Android app, package name com.blaybridge.tools. For privacy questions contact blaybridge@gmail.com. The website has a separate website privacy policy.

The app's document and image tools process your selected content on your device. No account is required. The app also connects to BlayBridge for announcements, feature settings and uses Google AdMob for advertising. These online services can process technical information as described below.

1. Documents, images and information you enter

You can choose files, take document photographs, draw or import signatures and enter invoice details, QR content or calculation values. The current app processes these materials locally to perform the tool you select. Its tool code does not upload document contents, photos, signatures, invoice details or calculation inputs to BlayBridge or include them in advertising requests.

The document scanner uses on-device text recognition. Selected content is not sent by the scanner to a remote text-recognition service. If you choose a cloud-backed file provider or another app to open, export or share a file, that provider handles its own transfers under its own policy.

2. Storage on your device

The app stores preferences such as favourites and onboarding status, saved invoice drafts and saved signatures locally. It may keep temporary processing files in its cache. Advertising frequency settings are also stored locally. You choose where exported files are saved using Android's document-saving interface.

Delete saved signatures or invoice drafts using the app's controls where provided. You can clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its private local data. Exported files and copies held by other apps or cloud providers remain until you delete them separately. Android automatic backup is disabled in the app's manifest.

3. Internet connections

The app fetches remote announcements, feature visibility and update notices from blaybridge.com/api/app-config. Our website infrastructure and hosting provider may receive IP addresses, request times and technical request information to deliver and protect these services. These requests do not include the contents of your selected documents or invoice drafts.

Update links open an external destination when you choose them. That destination may collect information under its own privacy policy. If you contact us, we receive the email address and other information you choose to send.

4. Google AdMob advertising

The app integrates Google's Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. Development builds use Google test advertisements. Release interstitial advertising uses the app's production ad unit and is requested only when Google's User Messaging Platform permits advertising requests. The current development banner uses a Google test unit.

Google's advertising SDK collects and shares technical information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. This includes IP address, which can indicate approximate location; device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID where available; interactions such as app launches, taps and ad or video views; and diagnostic and performance information. Test advertising can also involve network and technical data processing. The advertising SDK does not receive your document contents from the app's tool code.

Advertising services can store or access information on your device. Depending on your choices, location, device settings and the advertising configuration, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or limited. Non-personalised advertising can still involve technical data processing.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

In release builds, Google's consent platform checks whether a consent message is required before requesting interstitial ads. Where a message is required and configured, it lets you review available choices. Where available, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app to revisit your advertising preferences. This entry appears when Google's consent platform requires privacy options.

You can also manage or delete your advertising ID through your device's Android advertising privacy settings, where supported. Changing preferences does not remove data already processed. Some tools work offline, while advertising and remote announcements require internet access.

6. Permissions and device access

The app uses internet and network-state access for online services and advertising. File selection and saving use Android's system interfaces. Document photographs and signature photographs use an external camera app through Android's camera interface. You choose the files and photographs made available to the app. The current app does not request precise location, contacts, microphone, call-log or SMS access.

7. Sharing and service providers

BlayBridge does not sell your documents, signatures or invoice contents. Google processes advertising data as described in its policies. Hosting providers process technical requests to operate and protect BlayBridge services. If you share content through another app or open an external link, the selected service's practices apply. Advertising may involve Google's advertising partners as explained in the advertising privacy message and Google's policies.

8. Retention and security

Private local data remains on your device until removed by you, by the app's cleanup behaviour or by Android. Exported files remain in the destination you select. Contact messages and technical service records are kept as needed to respond to enquiries, operate and secure services or meet applicable obligations. Google's retention practices apply to data it processes.

App connections to BlayBridge use HTTPS. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Local files and saved signatures depend on your device's protections; the app does not provide a separate encrypted document vault. Protect your device and only process content you are authorised to use. No security measure can guarantee complete protection.

9. Your privacy requests

For questions or requests concerning personal information held by BlayBridge, email blaybridge@gmail.com. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent or complain to a relevant supervisory authority. We may need enough information to verify and handle your request.

BlayBridge cannot remotely retrieve or delete private documents or drafts stored only on your device. Use the local controls described above. Requests about Google's processing can be made through Google's privacy controls and contact channels. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable policies and safeguards.

10. Children

BlayBridge Tools is a general utility app and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through contact requests. If you believe a child has sent personal information to BlayBridge, contact us so we can review and address it. Parents and guardians should supervise use of document and signature tools.

11. Policy updates

We may revise this policy when the app's features, services or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Where required, additional notice or new consent will be provided.
