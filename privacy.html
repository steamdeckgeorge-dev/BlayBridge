Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

BlayBridge operates blaybridge.com and provides articles, digital resources and online tools. Questions about privacy can be sent to blaybridge@gmail.com.

Some tools may process files or text you submit to provide their requested function. Do not submit confidential documents unless you are comfortable with the tool's processing.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable law. Contact us at blaybridge@gmail.com. You may also manage cookies through your browser. Where consent is required, we will provide relevant choices before using optional technologies.

Updates
